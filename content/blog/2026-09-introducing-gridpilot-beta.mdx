GrydIn has opened private beta enrollment for **GridPilot**, our proprietary management console designed specifically for multi-agent enterprise workflows.

GridPilot solves the "black box" challenge of autonomous systems by providing visual state graphs, replayable execution histories, and mandatory human-in-the-loop checkpoints for high-risk operations.

<Callout type="tip">
Existing clients and partners can request early beta access through their GrydIn account lead or via our contact portal.
</Callout>

### Key Capabilities in Beta

- Real-time step inspection across multi-agent handoffs.
- Token consumption budgets with automatic runaway protection.
- One-click export of audit logs for compliance reviews.
