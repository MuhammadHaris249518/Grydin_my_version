GrydIn has established its primary engineering operations at **The Box Software Technology Park**, located at Plot 21, Street 36, I-9/2, Islamabad, Pakistan.

The technology park facility serves as the base for our core engineering and systems architecture teams, supporting clients across North America, Europe, Australia, and the Middle East.

<Callout type="info">
Our office location coordinates our global async-first delivery model, ensuring rapid diagnostic response times and continuous operational support.
</Callout>

### Facility Highlights

- Dedicated staging infrastructure for offline automation pipeline stress testing.
- High-availability redundant power and fiber network connectivity.
- Secure on-premise testing pods for enterprise data governance audits.
